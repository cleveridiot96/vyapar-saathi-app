# **App Name**: Vyapar Saathi

## Core Features:

- Dashboard: Provides a quick overview of key business metrics.
- Purchases: Records and manages incoming goods and purchase transactions.
- Sales: Enables creation and management of sales transactions.
- Location Transfer: Facilitates the transfer of stock between different warehouses.
- Inventory: Provides a view of current stock levels and inventory management features.
- Cash Book: Tracks daily cash flow and transactions.
- Outstanding Balances: Manages and displays receivables and payables.
- Stock Ledger: Provides party-wise stock balances.
- Accounts Ledger: Displays a full financial ledger per party.
- Lot Ledger: Traces any vakkal from purchase to sale.
- Profit Analysis: Analyzes profitability metrics.
- Financial Summary: Provides a business overview and key financial metrics.
- Payments: Records payments made.
- Receipts: Records receipts received.
- Daybook: Displays all daily entries and transactions.
- Masters: Manages all parties (customers, suppliers, etc.).
- Backup/Restore: Allows backing up and restoring app data.
- Self-Refactoring Code: Continuously refactors code to maintain optimal performance and speed.
- Bug Elimination: Automatically identifies and eliminates bugs to ensure a stable and reliable experience.
- Offline Operation: The entire app operates 100% offline, with no data exchange over the internet.
- Data Validations: All forms feature robust data validations to prevent errors and ensure data integrity.
- Intelligent Features: A cool and intelligent excel-like assistant feature that maps, suggests, and provides shortcuts for sales, purchases, cashbook, receipts, and payments without interfering with existing browser or OS shortcuts, and is on the free tier.
- Intelligent Caching: Utilizes a sophisticated caching system that reasons about the information it stores to render quickly.
- Stock Validation: Ensures that sales do not exceed available stock levels.
- Prorated Expense Calculation: Accurately distributes sale-related expenses across individual items to determine net profit per item.
